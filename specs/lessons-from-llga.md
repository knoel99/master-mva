## Rules carried over unchanged

1. One long page per course, numbered chapters `## N. Title {#chN}`, boxes `::: def / thm / prop / proof / ex / note / key / recap`.
2. Each chapter opens with one or two plain sentences saying what problem it solves; symbols defined before use.
3. Session plan, levels 1–3, self-study line, 100–320 prose words per in-class hour.
4. Exercises graded ★ / ★★ / ★★★ and sorted.
5. Airy prose: ≤ 100 words and ≤ 6 inline formulas per block, no inline formula over 50 TeX characters.
6. No invented course codes; facts of the fiche come from the official sources only.
7. The site is independent: no institutional branding, a “What is this website?” section on the home page.

## What the MVA site adds

- A machine-readable catalogue (`data/catalog-2026-2027.json`) built from master-mva.com and the Université Paris-Saclay programme table, read by the skeleton generator, the home generator and the gates.
- A **Projects and assessment** section on every science page and an end-of-year projects page (`specs/projects-and-assessment.md`), also added to the LLGA notes in the same round.
- Track and route buttons whose example programmes are checked against the MVA validation rules.
