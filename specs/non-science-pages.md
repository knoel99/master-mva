# Contract — non-science pages (framings)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| ID | `non-science-pages` |
| Pages | `year-hospital-immersion`, `s2-regulating-ai`, `s2-reproducible-research-project`, `s3-research-internship` (kind `non-science` in the catalogue) |
| Origin | LLGA contract of the same name (management, languages, internships) |

These modules are a project, a law-and-policy seminar or the internship: they get a **framing page**, not a course. Sections: `## Contents`, `## Introduction and overview {#intro}` (fiche with the official facts), `## What the module asks {#asks}`, `## How to prepare {#prepare}` (links to the science pages that help), `## Deliverables and assessment {#assessment}` (official rules only), `## Official sources {#refs}`. No session plan, no exercise quota, no projects quota. Airy prose applies.
