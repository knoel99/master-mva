## Tree and buttons

- Tree: refresher fortnight → semester 1 (Learning / Modelling / Opening) → semester 2 (same + project module) → across both semesters → research internship. Every catalogue page appears once.
- **Track buttons** (Santé, I&I, RR) highlight exactly the courses carrying the official track label.
- **Route buttons** (Learning theory, Vision and imaging, Generative AI and LLM) highlight an example programme of **exactly 8 modules** that satisfies the validation rules: ≥ 4 in semester 1, ≥ 3 in semester 2, ≥ 2 Learning, ≥ 2 Modelling, ≥ 40 ECTS. Each route shows a justification paragraph when selected. Routes are reading guidance, labelled as such.
- Pure CSS dimming (`.tree[data-path=…]`) + a short inline script; without JavaScript the full tree shows.
